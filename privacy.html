TikView Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 September 2026

TikView requests two TikTok permissions: user.info.basic, to know which creator authorized the app, and video.list, to read that creator's public video IDs and view counts.

We store the authorization token and the view counts on the operator's own computer and in a private Feishu spreadsheet. We do not sell this data, do not use it for advertising, and do not send it to any other company.

Creators can revoke TikView in TikTok under Settings, Security, and authorized apps. Revocation stops further collection. To ask for deletion of stored rows, contact the operator who sent the authorization link.
