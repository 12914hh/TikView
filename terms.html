TikView Terms of Service

Last updated: 19 September 2026

TikView is a private tool. It reads public TikTok video view counts only after a creator authorizes the app, then writes those numbers into the operator's private spreadsheet.

The tool does not post videos, send messages, buy ads, or share creator data with anyone else. A creator may revoke access at any time in TikTok settings. After revocation, TikView stops updating that creator's videos.

The operator uses the data only to track cooperation performance. This tool is not a public service and is not offered to outside customers.
